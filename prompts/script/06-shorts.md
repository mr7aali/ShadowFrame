# Prompt — Shorts from an Episode

**Step 6 of the script pipeline.** Input: the final locked script.

```text
From this locked episode script, write 3 YouTube Shorts scripts (9:16,
30–50 seconds, ~75–120 words each) that make viewers want to watch the full
episode. Do not spoil the twist or the culprit.

Script:
[PASTE FINAL SCRIPT]

Make one of each:
1. Teaser — the hook moment, ending on an unanswered question.
2. Clue — one clue shown up close; ask viewers what they think it means.
3. Character — a short moment that shows the protagonist's flaw or goal.

For each, give:
- On-screen hook text (max 6 words) for the first 2 seconds
- Narration script
- [VISUAL: ...] notes per shot (vertical framing)
- Closing line pointing to the full episode
```
