# Prompt — Thumbnail Image

Add the short text overlay in your editor afterwards, not in the AI image.

```text
Use the approved reference image for [CHARACTER NAME].
YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, [CHARACTER NAME] in a tight close-up on the
[left/right] third, expression: [EXPRESSION]. Behind them: [ONE DARK
LOCATION FROM THE EPISODE]. Strong contrast, dramatic rim lighting,
[BRAND COLOR] accent, clean empty space on the [opposite] side for text.
Readable at small mobile size. Must reflect a scene that is actually in the
episode. No text, no logos, no watermark, no gore.
```
