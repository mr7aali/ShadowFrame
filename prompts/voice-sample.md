# Prompt — Voice Design / Sample

Use with an AI voice tool that supports voice design from a text description. Confirm the plan allows commercial use. Never clone a real person's voice without permission.

## Voice description

```text
[NARRATOR / CHARACTER NAME] — [male/female/neutral], age impression [AGE],
neutral American accent. [Low/medium/high] pitch, [smooth/gravelly/warm/cold]
texture, [slow/measured/brisk] pace. Calm, controlled delivery that can rise
to tension without shouting. Suitable for a dark, cinematic crime-thriller
narration.
```

## Test lines (read these to approve the voice)

```text
The rain hadn't stopped in three days. Neither had the phone calls.

Detective [NAME] knew one thing for certain: someone in this town was lying.

"You're asking the wrong question," she said. "Ask me why I came back."

And when the lights went out, only one person in that room wasn't surprised.
```

Record the approved settings (model, stability, style, speed) in the [Character Bible](../templates/character-bible.md).
