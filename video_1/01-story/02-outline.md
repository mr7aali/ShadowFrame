# Step 2 — Outline (filled example)

**Tool:** Claude · **Template:** [prompts/script/02-outline.md](../../prompts/script/02-outline.md)

## Prompt sent

```text
Build a beat-by-beat outline for a 14-minute narrated YouTube episode of an
original fictional crime thriller.

Premise: The Clock That Stopped at 9:14. Pine Hollow, Minnesota, January
1996, during a blizzard. Walter Krane, 68, a watchmaker, is found dead at
the foot of his workshop stairs. His pocket watch is smashed and stopped at
9:14. His apprentice Tess set the watch herself to give herself an alibi.
Story shape: classic whodunit

Recurring cast (from the Character Bible — use exactly as written):
- Detective Mara Quinn — flaw: trusts her first instinct too fast.
  Father Frank Quinn, former county sheriff, died 20 years ago.
- Deputy Eli Booker — her partner; writes everything in a notebook.

[structure, word budget, and requirements exactly as in the template]
```

## Output — beats

**[0:00–0:30] Hook** — 41 clocks still ticking; one pocket watch stopped at 9:14; everyone believes that's the time of death, except Mara.

**[0:30–3:00] Setup**
- Pine Hollow, 1996. Mara's history with the town and her father. Her flaw.
- 9:41 p.m. 911 call during a blizzard.
- Drive in with Eli. **Clue 2 planted:** two lines of small boot prints, one sharp, one half filled with snow.
- Meet Tess (introduced early — culprit must appear early). Her story: "came back for my scarf."
- Walter at the foot of the stairs; looks like a slip.
- **Clue 1 planted:** the watch crown is pulled out. *Cliffhanger:* "Find out where everyone was at 9:14."

**[3:00–7:00] Investigation**
- Joyce: Ronnie argued with Walter around 8. Joyce has a spare key and wanted the building (suspect 2).
- Lawyer: Ronnie inherits everything.
- Ronnie lies ("asleep") — warm truck hood. Mara locks onto him (**flaw in action**).
- Dawn: Tess sweeping; brushes off the crown question. Foreshadow line: "a watch that runs too perfectly is hiding something."
- **Clue 3 (behavior):** Joyce says Tess kept asking the time. Joyce cleared by 11 customers.
- *Cliffhanger:* Mara underlines "perfect."

**[7:00–10:00] Complication**
- Ronnie arrested; the town is relieved.
- **Reversal:** Ronnie's alibi is real — illegal card game, seven witnesses. Mara's instinct was wrong.
- Father's line: "The first answer is usually the one somebody wants you to find."
- **Clue 4:** ledger page torn out cleanly.
- Tess appears, points at Ronnie again (missing Bergstrom watch).
- Tess's boots match **both** lines of prints. *Cliffhanger:* "The snow said she came back twice."

**[10:00–13:00] Climax and reveal**
- Mara invites Tess to wind the clocks at 9 p.m.
- Pencil rubbing reveals Walter's last note: "...All handled by T.H. Call sheriff."
- Tess denies it. Mara explains the whole method (prints, crown, asking Joyce the time). Lab print on the crown.
- Tess confesses: mother's care-home costs; she grabbed his arm. Eli was by the door.

**[13:00–14:00] Ending**
- Ronnie released, sells the clocks; Tess pleads guilty.
- Shoebox of stolen movements. The last one: "For Frank. Never late."
- **Chilling last line:** Mara watched them bury her father with that watch.
