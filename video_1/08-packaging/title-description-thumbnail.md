## Prompt sent to Claude

```text
Write YouTube packaging for this original fictional crime-thriller episode.
Audience: adults in the U.S., UK, Canada, and Australia.

Script summary: In a 1996 Minnesota blizzard, old watchmaker Walter Krane is
found dead at the bottom of his workshop stairs, his pocket watch stopped at
9:14. Detective Mara Quinn suspects the nephew, who inherits everything, but
his alibi holds. Clues (a pulled watch crown, two sets of boot prints, a torn
ledger page) reveal the apprentice Tess staged the watch to give herself an
alibi. TWIST TO AVOID: Tess is the killer; the final watch belongs to Mara's
dead father.

Give me:
1. 5 title options, under 60 characters, clear hook, no clickbait.
2. A description whose first two lines hook without spoiling the twist,
   then episode/series info and a fiction notice.
3. 3 thumbnail text options of 3–4 words each.
4. A pinned-comment question that invites real discussion.
```
