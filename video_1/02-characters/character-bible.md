## MARA QUINN — protagonist (recurring)

```text
ROLE:            Sheriff's detective, Pine Hollow County Sheriff's Office
AGE RANGE:       Late 30s
STORY GOAL:      Prove she belongs back in the town her father ran
FEAR:            Becoming her father — or never measuring up to him
STRENGTH:        Notices small physical details others walk past
FLAW:            Trusts her first instinct too fast
BACKSTORY:       Grew up in Pine Hollow. Father Frank Quinn was county
                 sheriff for 20 years and died in 1976. She left at 18,
                 worked in Minneapolis, came back in 1995 for reasons she
                 doesn't discuss.
SECRET:          She left Minneapolis after a case she got wrong.
MORAL BOUNDARY:  Won't plant or bend evidence — even for someone guilty.
CHARACTER ARC:   Learns to distrust the "first answer."

FACE:            Pale skin with faint freckles, grey-green eyes, thin
                 vertical scar through her LEFT eyebrow
HAIR:            Dark brown, shoulder length, always in a low ponytail
BUILD / HEIGHT:  Lean, 5'7"
MAIN OUTFIT:     Long charcoal wool coat, navy turtleneck, dark jeans,
                 brown leather gloves, mustard-yellow knit scarf
ACCESSORIES:     Silver wristwatch on LEFT wrist, small spiral notebook
POSTURE:         Still, watchful; tilts her head when something is wrong
EXPRESSIONS:     Narrowed eyes, tight half-smile, rarely laughs

VOICE:           Narrator voices her lines (see voice-setup.md)
DIALOGUE STYLE:  Short, flat, direct. Questions, not speeches.

FIXED PROMPT DESCRIPTION:
Detective Mara Quinn, a lean woman in her late 30s, pale skin with faint
freckles, grey-green eyes, thin scar through her left eyebrow, dark brown
hair in a low ponytail, long charcoal wool coat, mustard-yellow knit scarf,
brown leather gloves

APPROVED REFERENCE FILES: mara_front.png, mara_34.png, mara_profile.png,
                          mara_fullbody.png
DO NOT CHANGE:   scar side (left), scarf color, ponytail, coat color
```
