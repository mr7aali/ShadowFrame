# Image-to-Video Prompts — EP001 (10 animated shots)

**Tool:** an image-to-video generator (e.g. Runway, Kling, Google Veo, Luma, Pika).
**How:** upload the approved still (`shot_XX.png`) as the **first frame**, paste the prompt, generate **5 seconds**. Make 2–3 takes and keep the best.
**Save as:** `05-generated-video/shot_XX.mp4`

**Rules for every clip:** small, slow motion only. Big movement causes face drift and glitches. If a clip looks wrong, use the still image with a Ken Burns move instead.

---

**01 — clock shop**
```text
Pendulums of the antique clocks swing gently back and forth, dust drifts slowly through the lamp light, the camera pushes in very slowly. No people. Keep everything else still and consistent.
```

**02 — clock faces**
```text
The second hands on the antique clock faces tick forward, pendulums glint below, slow smooth pan from left to right. Keep the clock faces sharp and consistent.
```

**04 — Main Street blizzard**
```text
Heavy snow falls and swirls in the wind, the traffic light blinks yellow, the patrol car's lights flash red and blue softly, slow camera pull-back. No people walking. Keep buildings stable.
```

**11 — patrol car**
```text
Windshield wipers sweep slowly across the glass, snow streaks past in the headlights, the woman driving keeps her eyes on the road, the man beside her writes in his notebook. Subtle movement only, faces stay the same.
```

**13 — boot prints**
```text
Snowflakes fall gently through the flashlight beam onto the boot prints, the light moves very slightly as if handheld. The prints stay exactly the same.
```

**28 — warm truck hood**
```text
A thin wisp of steam rises from the truck hood, melting snow slowly slides and drips, the gloved hand stays still, the flashlight beam wavers slightly.
```

**51 — winding the clock**
```text
The young woman's hands turn the small brass key in the clock smoothly, two slow turns, then the pendulum starts to swing. Hands stay natural with five fingers, no other movement.
```

**57 — setting the watch (flashback)**
```text
The fingers slowly turn the watch crown and the hands move to 9:14 and stop. Very slow, precise motion. Keep the fingers natural and the watch shape consistent.
```

**59 — standoff among the clocks**
```text
All the pendulums swing gently in rhythm, the two women stand completely still facing each other, the camera pulls back very slowly. No facial movement.
```

**66 — cemetery ending**
```text
Snow falls softly, the lantern flame flickers, the woman stands still holding the watch, the camera pulls back slowly and rises a little. Calm and eerie.
```
