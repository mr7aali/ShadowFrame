## Tricky shots — tips

- **Shots 03, 18, 58 (watch at 9:14):** AI often gets clock hands wrong. If 4–5 tries fail, photograph any real watch set to 9:14 on a grey vest, or fix the hands in Photoshop/Photopea.
- **Shot 16 (body):** never show the face or injuries. Show only a hand, cracked glasses, and the stairs. This keeps the video advertiser-friendly.
- **Shots 13 / 48 (boot prints):** generate a few versions and pick the one where the two lines of prints are clearly different.
