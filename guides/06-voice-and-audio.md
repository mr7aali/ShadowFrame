# 06 — Voice, Music, and Sound Design

Related prompts: [voice sample](../prompts/voice-sample.md) · [narration direction](../prompts/narration-direction.md)

## Narrator voice
- Clear, neutral, American-sounding voice — yours or an AI voice.
- Pick one narrator voice for the channel and never change it between episodes.

## Voice setup
- [ ] Choose voice type, age impression, accent, pitch, texture, speed.
- [ ] Create a pronunciation list for names and locations.
- [ ] Generate and approve a short voice sample.
- [ ] Confirm the voice provider permits commercial use.
- [ ] Never clone another person's voice without clear permission and rights.
- [ ] Record the voice model/settings in the Character Bible.

## Voice production
- [ ] Generate narration only after the script is locked.
- [ ] Assign one consistent voice to each speaking character.
- [ ] Use emotion and delivery direction where supported.
- [ ] Keep pronunciation consistent.
- [ ] Check timing against the visuals.
- [ ] Regenerate lines that sound robotic, rushed, or emotionally wrong.

## Music and sound effects
- [ ] Music supports tension without overpowering the voice.
- [ ] Location ambience (rain, traffic, room tone, wind, footsteps).
- [ ] Sound effects only when they match visible action.
- [ ] Use silence intentionally before key reveals.
- [ ] Duck music under narration and dialogue.
- [ ] Consistent loudness; no clipping, hiss, or harsh peaks.
- [ ] Keep proof of licenses for all music and SFX.
- [ ] No popular commercial songs without the required rights.
