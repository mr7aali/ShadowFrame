# 09 — YouTube Policy, Copyright, and Monetization Safety

Fiction removes the need for real-world fact-checking, but it does **not** remove copyright, disclosure, or advertiser-friendly rules.

- [ ] Story, script, characters, dialogue, and editing are meaningfully original.
- [ ] No mass-produced, near-identical videos with only names or images changed.
- [ ] Add your own human edit and style to every AI-generated script.
- [ ] No other creators' footage, movie scenes, music, or artwork without permission or a valid legal basis.
- [ ] Don't assume AI-generated means copyright-free or safe for commercial use.
- [ ] Check the terms of every AI image, video, voice, music, and editing service.
- [ ] Keep invoices, plan details, license terms, and permission records.
- [ ] No impersonation of real people; no synthetic media that could mislead about real events.
- [ ] Characters and places are fictional; label stories clearly as fiction.
- [ ] Follow YouTube's current altered/synthetic content disclosure requirements.
- [ ] Follow advertiser-friendly guidelines: graphic violence, gore, shocking imagery, and sensationalized crime can limit ads.
- [ ] Never promise monetization; eligibility depends on current YouTube Partner Program requirements.
- [ ] Recheck YouTube policies before launch and whenever they change.
