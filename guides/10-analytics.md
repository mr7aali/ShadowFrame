# 10 — Analytics and Improvement

Template: [episode scorecard](../templates/episode-scorecard.md)

Review each video after publishing:
- [ ] Impressions and click-through rate (CTR).
- [ ] First 30-second retention.
- [ ] Exact timestamps where viewers leave.
- [ ] Average view duration and percentage viewed.
- [ ] Traffic sources and geography (U.S. vs. UK/Canada/Australia vs. others).
- [ ] Comments: confusion, story requests, character feedback.
- [ ] Thumbnail/title performance over time; change only with a reason.
- [ ] Strongest and weakest scene.
- [ ] One improvement for the next episode.
- [ ] Judge patterns across several videos, never one upload.
