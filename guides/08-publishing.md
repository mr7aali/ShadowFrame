# 08 — Thumbnail, Title, Metadata, and Publishing

Related prompts: [title & description](../prompts/title-and-description.md) · [thumbnail](../prompts/thumbnail.md)

## Thumbnail and title
- [ ] One clear visual idea: one strong face, one dark location, 3–4 word phrase.
- [ ] Main subject readable at small mobile size.
- [ ] No misleading imagery or scenes that aren't in the video.
- [ ] Write 3–5 title options with a clear hook (e.g. "The Detective Who Never Closed Case #47") and choose the clearest.
- [ ] Check spelling of title, description, and thumbnail text.

## Description and metadata
- [ ] First two lines explain the story without spoiling the twist.
- [ ] Include: "This story is a work of fiction. Any resemblance to real people or events is coincidental."
- [ ] Playlist and episode number.
- [ ] Accurate captions/subtitles.
- [ ] End screens and cards where useful.

## Upload checklist
- [ ] Video is original and all assets have the right licenses.
- [ ] Correct audience setting (not "made for kids").
- [ ] Altered/synthetic-content disclosure completed honestly per YouTube's current rules.
- [ ] Copyright checks in YouTube Studio passed.
- [ ] Visibility, schedule, playlist, language, subtitles confirmed.
- [ ] Upload privately/unlisted first and inspect the processed video.
- [ ] Preview the watch page.
- [ ] Pin a relevant comment if useful; no misleading engagement bait.
- [ ] Save final title, description, thumbnail, upload date, and license records.

## Upload timing
- Test evenings and weekends in U.S. time zones (Eastern and Pacific); adjust using YouTube Analytics.
- Since you're in a different time zone from your audience, schedule uploads in advance instead of posting live.
